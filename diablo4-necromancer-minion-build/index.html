Necromancer Minion Build

Blight Summoner · Shadowblight · Book of the Dead · Command an undead army that never stops.

Shadow Summoning Levelling PvE ✓ HC Viable WT1 → WT4

Best ForCampaign, levelling, WT1–WT4, Hardcore

The Necromancer Minion build is one of Diablo 4's most consistently powerful levelling archetypes. You command a permanent army — up to eight skeletal warriors, four skeletal mages, and a golem — while your role is to curse enemies, cast Blight puddles, and let your undead do the killing. The damage ceiling rises steeply once the Shadowblight key passive is active, and the build's inherent tankiness (the minions absorb most incoming damage) makes it an excellent choice for Hardcore.

The core loop is straightforward: curse with Decrepify, drop a Blight puddle, let minions proc Shadowblight stacks (ten instances of Darkness damage → massive bonus shadow hit), generate corpses, use Army of the Dead on cooldown. Every layer of the build reinforces every other — cursed enemies take more damage via Amplify Damage, minions attack faster near you via Inspiring Leader, and Blight puddles the Golem creates via the Plunging Darkness aspect mean you barely have to cast at all in routine packs.

This guide covers three variants: the standard Blight Summoner (best overall damage and Shadowblight synergy), the Army Commander (maximum minion uptime and Corpse Explosion burst), and the Blood Summoner (self-healing focus, ideal for Hardcore).

Strengths

  • Extremely low APM — minions do the work
  • Minions absorb incoming damage — very durable
  • Shadowblight spikes massive damage every 10 shadow hits
  • Decrepify reduces boss damage dealt to you
  • Scales smoothly through every World Tier
  • Excellent Hardcore viability
  • Army of the Dead nukes most campaign bosses

Weaknesses

  • Minions die to AoE ground effects — reposition needed
  • Corpse generation required for several skills
  • Hellbent Commander requires staying near minions
  • Slightly passive — some players find it low-engagement
  • Army of the Dead has a long cooldown between uses

Build Variants

Three approaches to the Minion Necromancer. The stat spread and Book of the Dead choices are nearly identical across all three — the difference is in which secondary skills you prioritise and how you engage with the corpse loop.

Blight Summoner — Standard (Recommended)

The strongest overall version. Blight acts as your primary damage skill and Shadowblight primer. Every cast leaves a shadow puddle that minions fight inside, each hit incrementing the Shadowblight stack. At ten stacks the next shadow damage instance explodes for bonus damage — against bosses this happens multiple times per fight. Decrepify ensures enemies take more damage from both you and your minions simultaneously.

Army Commander — Maximum Minion Count

Leans further into the Corpse Explosion loop. Swap Blight for Bone Spear if you want more personal damage, or keep Blight and add Corpse Tendrils to group enemies tightly for Corpse Explosion detonation. Prioritises personal survivability without sacrificing minion damage. Abhorrent Decrepify already heals you when cursed enemies die — this variant stacks on Blood Mist for an emergency invulnerability tool and Death's Embrace at maximum rank for near-permanent damage reduction in melee range. Minions still deal the bulk of damage. This is the recommended variant for Hardcore characters until World Tier 3 aspect options are fully assembled.

The Shadowblight Trigger Explained

Shadowblight counts every instance of Darkness damage — Blight ticks, Shadow Mage attacks, Blighted Corpse Explosion pools, and the Golem's Blight puddle (via Plunging Darkness aspect) all count independently. In a full pack with four Shadow Mages attacking, Shadowblight triggers every 2–3 seconds. The bonus hit deals approximately 30% of your shadow skill damage as an additional burst — it is not capped and benefits from Amplify Damage.

Book of the Dead

The Book of the Dead is unique to the Necromancer — it lets you choose the type and behaviour of each minion category, or sacrifice them for a passive bonus. For this build, do not sacrifice any minion type. The combined damage output of a full army vastly exceeds any sacrifice bonus at every stage of levelling.

Skeletal Warriors
Skirmishers ✓ (Recommended)
Best for Levelling

Skirmishers attack 10% faster than Warriors and deal bonus damage against Healthy enemies. Since most encounters begin with full-HP targets, the bonus fires immediately. More attacks per second also means more Shadowblight stack accumulation per minute.

Alternative — Warriors: Higher single-hit damage, better for one-on-one boss fights. Swap here if you find Skirmishers dying on WT3+ elite packs. The DPS difference is marginal.

Do not pick Defenders — their damage output is too low for the stat investment required.

Skeletal Mages
Shadow Mages ✓ (Recommended)
Best for Levelling

Shadow Mages deal AoE shadow damage with each attack — every hit counts toward Shadowblight stacks. Four Shadow Mages in a pack can push the Shadowblight counter to ten in under two seconds, triggering the bonus hit before enemies even react. Their AoE naturally tags every enemy in a cluster.

Alternative — Cold Mages: If you are dying frequently, Cold Mages freeze and Chill enemies, buying time. The trade-off is losing shadow damage contributions to Shadowblight. Switch back once survivability improves.

Golem
Bone Golem ✓ (Recommended)
Best for Levelling

The Bone Golem is the most durable option and generates Bone Splinters projectiles when it takes damage, adding to your own Essence generation passively. Its active ability — a ground slam — is the trigger for the Aspect of Plunging Darkness, which creates a Blight puddle at the slam location. This converts every Golem slam into an extra Shadowblight primer.

Alternative — Blood Golem (HC): Drains life from nearby enemies and channels it to you and nearby allies. For Hardcore characters, the sustain is valuable. Loses the Plunging Darkness synergy.

Sacrifice — When Not To

Sacrificing a minion type gives a passive stat bonus — e.g. sacrificing all Skeletal Warriors gives +20% Critical Strike Chance. At no point during levelling (1–50) does a sacrifice bonus outperform keeping the minion alive. The combined DPS of eight skeletons, four mages, and a golem is far greater. Reserve sacrifice builds for fully geared endgame specialisations.

Stats & Levelling

Diablo 4's Necromancer scales through Intelligence (skill damage) and Willpower (resource generation and Overpower damage). Unlike a pure caster build, the Minion Summoner benefits greatly from gear stats that directly boost the army — minion damage percentages, minion attack speed, and skill ranks are often more impactful than raw primary stat increases, especially post-level 35.

Stat Priority Summary

Gear affixes (in priority order): + Ranks to Raise Skeleton → + Ranks to Golem → Minion Damage % → Minion Attack Speed % → Maximum Life → Intelligence → Cooldown Reduction (for Army of the Dead) → Lucky Hit Chance. Resistance caps at 70% — get there before pushing World Tier 3.

Levelling Roadmap

🌱 Early — Levels 1–25

Focus: Bone Splinters → Blight → Decrepify → Grim Harvest → Fueled by Death

Your army forms automatically as you unlock Raise Skeleton and Golem through skill tree points. Prioritise Blight upgrades and Grim Harvest to establish the Essence loop. Decrepify should be active on every elite and boss encounter from level 12 onward. Shadowblight is still out of reach — rely on minion auto-attacks and Blight puddles for damage.

⚔️ Mid — Levels 26–45

Focus: Shadowblight → Inspiring Leader → Hellbent Commander → Army of the Dead

This is the build's power spike. Once Shadowblight is active, damage jumps noticeably — you will see enemies that previously required a full fight dying in one Army of the Dead + Blight combo. Stack Inspiring Leader and Hellbent Commander immediately. Stay within melee range of your minions for maximum damage. Push into World Tier 2 around level 30 if the campaign allows.

💀 Late — Levels 45–50+

Focus: Aspect acquisition → resistance capping → Paragon board setup

Run the Codex dungeons listed in the Aspects section before hitting level 50. The Plunging Darkness and Damned aspects transform the build. At 50, complete the Capstone Dungeon to unlock World Tier 3 — do this at the earliest point your gear allows. Paragon boards (post-50) provide major power gains. See the Paragon section.

Aspects

Aspects are Legendary powers imprinted on gear. Codex of Power aspects (earned by completing specific dungeons) can be re-imprinted unlimited times at the Occultist — run the dungeons listed below before level 50 so you always have these available. Drop aspects (found on Legendary items) can only be used once.

How Aspects Work

Complete the dungeon once to permanently unlock the Aspect in your Codex of Power. Visit the Occultist to imprint it onto a matching gear slot. You can reimprint the same Aspect onto higher-level gear as you upgrade — the dungeon only needs to be completed once per account.

Gear & Gems

While levelling, gear quality changes frequently enough that you should not over-invest in upgrading any single piece. Focus on getting the right affixes — Minion Damage %, skill ranks, and Maximum Life — and upgrade when you find a better base. Gems are cost-effective power that applies to every piece of gear from the moment you socket them.

Gem Priorities

Socket LocationGemBenefit
WeaponsRubyBonus Overpower damage. Overpower hits scale with Bonus Life (Max Life - Base Life), and your minions trigger Overpower through your Lucky Hit procs. A solid damage gain throughout levelling.
ArmourRubyIncreases Maximum Life directly. Straightforward survivability — the most important gem in armour slots until you hit the resistance cap.
Jewellery (Rings/Amulet)DiamondGrants All Resistances. Getting all resistances to 70% before World Tier 3 prevents one-shots from elemental damage. Diamond in all three jewellery slots is the fastest path there.

Weapon Choice

Use a two-handed scythe as your primary weapon — this is the Necromancer's signature weapon type and rolls the widest range of Necromancer-specific affixes, including Minion Damage %, Intelligence, and Shadow Skill Damage. A well-rolled two-handed scythe beats a two-weapon setup for this build.

Reroll Priority at the Occultist

When upgrading gear at the Occultist, prioritise rerolling affixes in this order: replace any Strength/Dexterity rolls first (they do nothing for Necromancer) → then replace generic damage % with Minion Damage % → then fill in Maximum Life if the slot is missing it. Do not waste gold upgrading gear before level 40 unless an item has near-perfect affixes.

Paragon Boards

Paragon Boards unlock at level 50. Each board is a grid of nodes — Normal (minor stats), Magic (larger bonuses), Rare (powerful affixes), and Legendary (unique build-defining effects). Glyphs socketed into Glyph Sockets scale based on how many surrounding nodes of the correct type are filled.

Paragon Priority for Summoner

Your primary targets are nodes that boost Minion Damage, Shadow Damage, and Summoning Skill damage. Path towards Legendary nodes on each board first — they carry the most power per Paragon point — then fill in surrounding Magic and Rare nodes to empower your Glyph.

Combat Rotation

The Minion Summoner has one of the lowest active-skill rotations in the game. Most damage is passive. Your job is to cast the right things at the right time, then stay close to your army and let Hellbent Commander do the rest.

Standard Pack Rotation

  1. 1Decrepify — cast this on the nearest enemy group before engaging. Slows the pack, activates Amplify Damage for all subsequent minion attacks, and triggers Abhorrent healing when the enemies die.
  2. 2Blight — place one or two puddles where the enemies are or will be. Paranormal Blight further slows targets inside the pool, extending the time they take Darkness damage and accumulate Shadowblight stacks.
  3. 3Golem Slam — activate the Golem's ability if it is off cooldown. This triggers the Aspect of Plunging Darkness, creating an additional Blight pool. One button press gives you a free extra Blight.
  4. 4Stay close to your minions — position yourself inside the army. Hellbent Commander (+30% minion damage) and Death's Embrace (damage reduction) both require close proximity. Do not kite away from the fight.
  5. 5Bone Splinters — cast when Essence is below 50 or when waiting for cooldowns. Generates Essence, triggers Inspiring Leader (attack speed buff to minions), and with Initiated, fires bonus on the first four casts after each corpse pickup.
  6. 6Corpse Explosion — when corpses appear from killed enemies or Hewed Flesh procs, detonate them. Blighted Corpse Explosion creates additional Blight pools. Grim Harvest fires on each corpse consumed, and Fueled by Death boosts your damage for 3 seconds after each consumption.

Elite / Boss Rotation

  1. 1Decrepify — mandatory before the fight begins. Reduces boss damage output and activates Amplify Damage.
  2. 2Army of the Dead — use immediately. The Supreme version makes your existing minions temporarily invulnerable, preventing AoE wipes. The summoned skeleton wave adds burst damage and body-blocks the boss.
  3. 3Blight + Golem Slam — stack Blight puddles on the boss's position. Two Blight casts plus a Golem Slam creates three overlapping Darkness zones. Shadowblight will fire constantly.
  4. 4Maintain Decrepify — reapply when the duration expires. A boss without Decrepify hits significantly harder and your healing from Abhorrent stops.
  5. 5Bone Splinters between casts — fill downtime with Bone Splinters to keep Essence full and Inspiring Leader active.
  6. 6Blood Mist (HC only) — if below 30% health, use Blood Mist immediately. The brief invulnerability window lets Abhorrent healing and Decrepify restore HP before re-engaging.
The Hellbent Rule

Hellbent Commander is the most important passive in this build — but only while you are physically close to your minions. Most players instinctively back away from dangerous enemies. Resist this. Position behind your Golem (which tanks the brunt of hits), not behind yourself. You lose up to 30% minion damage every second you spend out of range.

World Tier Progression

Diablo 4's World Tier system gates content difficulty and loot quality. The Minion Summoner handles each transition well, but timing matters — pushing too early results in one-shots; pushing too late leaves XP and loot on the table.

World Tier 1 → World Tier 2 (Adventurer → Veteran)

Switch to World Tier 2 as soon as you feel comfortable — around level 15–20 for most players. WT2 increases XP gain by 20% and drops better loot. The Minion Summoner is strong enough to make this transition earlier than most other builds because your army absorbs a significant portion of incoming damage. If your minions are dying frequently, stay in WT1 until your gear improves.

Checkpoint: Can your Golem survive an elite pack without dying? Can you clear a dungeon without your skeleton army being wiped? If yes, switch immediately.

World Tier 2 → World Tier 3 (Veteran → Nightmare)

Complete the Capstone Dungeon: Cathedral of Light (Kyovashad, Fractured Peaks) at level 50 or close to it. This dungeon has a recommended level of 50 and gates World Tier 3. The boss, the Curator, hits hard — ensure Decrepify is active at all times and use Army of the Dead immediately on the final phase. Your minions should handle most of the damage if properly buffed.

Before attempting: Get the Plunging Darkness and Damned aspects imprinted. Cap your resistances with Diamond gems in jewellery. Ensure your Army of the Dead cooldown is under 90 seconds via Cooldown Reduction gear.

WT3 unlocks: Nightmare Dungeons, Helltide events, Whispers of the Dead, and Sacred/Ancestral item drops. Your Paragon board also becomes available at level 50.

World Tier 3 → World Tier 4 (Nightmare → Torment)

This requires completing the Capstone Dungeon: Fallen Temple (Dry Steppes). Recommended at level 70+. By this point, your Paragon boards should have at least 20–30 nodes filled, including the Deadraiser Glyph upgraded through Nightmare Dungeons. Your aspects should be imprinted on Sacred or Ancestral gear with item power 600+.

The Minion Summoner in WT4 benefits enormously from Ancestral items with triple Minion Damage rolls. Farm Helltide and Nightmare Dungeons (Tier 20+) to accumulate Sacred gear before attempting the Capstone. Once in WT4, Uber Uniques and the highest power Legendary drops become available.

HC note: Do not push to WT4 until you have confirmed Army of the Dead cooldown under 60 seconds via gear and Paragon nodes. One long boss fight without that ultimate ready can end a run.

Boss Tips

The Minion Summoner is one of the best boss-killing archetypes in the game during levelling — your army stays on the boss while you manage curses and cooldowns. The biggest threat is AoE ground effects that wipe the skeleton army. Watch for these and reposition the Golem to force minion clustering away from hazard zones.

Campaign — Frequent Encounter
The Butcher

Diablo 4's random dungeon ambush boss. Appears without warning. Your response: immediately cast Decrepify, activate Army of the Dead, and drop two Blight puddles. The Golem will tank him. If your gear is weak and he begins killing skeletons faster than they respawn, use Blood Mist (HC) or simply kite in circles while the army respawns. He has a hard time cornering you if you keep moving. He despawns after a timer — surviving is a win.

Campaign — Act Boss
Vhenard (Act 1 Final)

Two-phase fight with spreading shadow puddles in phase two. Position your Golem at the edge of the arena away from puddle spawn points. Your Shadow Mages naturally deal shadow damage — Shadowblight will proc frequently here. Army of the Dead is the phase transition tool: use it immediately when she enters phase two to burst through the HP threshold before her AoE escalates.

Campaign — Act Boss
Elias (Act 3 Final)

A multi-phase fight with a significant add spawn in phase two. This is where the Minion Summoner shines — your army immediately switches targets to incoming adds while Hellbent Commander maintains the damage buff on Elias simultaneously. Use Decrepify on Elias, not the adds. Amplify Damage on the boss matters more than slowing the adds.

Campaign — Final Boss
Lilith

Two-phase fight requiring mechanics. Phase one: platform is narrow — keep the Golem positioned centrally so the skeleton army clusters around it rather than falling off edges. Phase two introduces wing sweeps that can wipe the army; use Army of the Dead immediately to restore invulnerability and restack minions. Decrepify should be refreshed every 8 seconds. Stay aggressive — this fight is not designed to be kited.

World Boss
Ashava the Pestilent

Avoid the poison swirls — they kill skeletons quickly. Position your army at Ashava's rear legs where most AoEs don't reach. Your role: maintain Decrepify on Ashava constantly, drop Blight on her position, and reserve Army of the Dead for the enrage phase. You contribute meaningfully even in a group because Amplify Damage applies to all player damage against the cursed target.

Capstone Dungeon
The Curator (WT2 → WT3 Gate)

Quick Tips & Reference

A condensed reference for the Necromancer Minion build — everything you need to know without reading the full guide.

Never Sacrifice Your Minions

The sacrifice bonuses are designed to support minion-free Necromancer builds. For the Summoner, keeping all three minion types (Warriors, Mages, Golem) active is always the correct choice during levelling. The combined army DPS exceeds any passive bonus at every stage.

Decrepify Is Not Optional

Apply Decrepify before every single elite pack and boss. It is not just a damage buff — Abhorrent Decrepify heals you when cursed enemies die, making it a critical survivability tool. Never engage a dangerous encounter without it active.

Stay Inside Your Army

Hellbent Commander grants +30% minion damage only when you are physically close to your minions. The instinct to back away from dangerous enemies costs you a third of your army's damage. Position behind the Golem and fight close.

Shadowblight Counts Minion Hits

Every shadow damage hit from your Shadow Mages, Blight puddles, Golem slams (via Plunging Darkness), and Blighted Corpse Explosions counts toward the Shadowblight stack of ten. You do not need to manage this — just ensure all sources are active.

Run Codex Dungeons Early

Complete Rimescar Cavern (Plunging Darkness) and Inferno (Aspect of the Damned) before level 40. These two aspects alone transform the build's damage output. The dungeons take 5–10 minutes each and unlock permanent Codex entries you can reimprint on better gear later.

Diamond Gems in Jewellery

All Resistances cap at 70%. Until you hit that cap across all damage types, Diamond gems in all jewellery slots are the highest-priority quality-of-life investment. One-shots from elemental damage drop off sharply once resistances are capped.

Army of the Dead — Save It for Bosses

Do not use Army of the Dead on regular packs. It is a long cooldown (around 90 seconds base) designed for elite or boss encounters. The invulnerability window from Prime AotD is a wipe-prevention tool — use it when the boss begins an AoE sequence that would otherwise destroy your army.

Minion Attack Speed on Gloves

Minion Attack Speed % is one of the most powerful affixes for this build — more attacks means more Shadowblight stacks per second and more Inspiring Leader procs. Gloves are the primary slot that rolls Minion Attack Speed. Prioritise this over raw Intelligence on that slot.

Raise Skeleton Ranks on Helmet & Offhand

Every rank of Raise Skeleton increases the number of skeletons summoned and boosts their damage. These ranks stack between your Helmet and Offhand Focus. A +6 total roll across both pieces meaningfully increases your army's size and output. Always check these slots when upgrading.
