What Is Blood Guides?

Why players trust Blood Guides for tested builds and strategies

No Fluff. Just Guides.

Every guide is written by someone who has actually played the build — tested in PvE, checked against bosses, verified with accurate item locations. No padding, no vague advice.

Every Game. Every Playstyle.

Starting with Elden Ring and expanding across all genres — Soulslikes, action RPGs, shooters, strategy, and beyond. If a game has a meta worth optimising, we'll cover it.

Built for Readability

Stat tables, item cards, variant tabs, boss notes, quick reference tables. Guides are structured so you can find exactly what you need — whether you want the full explanation or just the items.

Always Up to Date

Guides are reviewed after major patches. Build recommendations reflect current patch viability — if something gets nerfed, the guide says so and tells you what to run instead.
