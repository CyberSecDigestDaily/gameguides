Samurai Bleed Build

The Sanguine Ronin — Unabridged Guide for Elden Ring & Shadow of the Erdtree

Dexterity / Arcane Patch 1.10 Viable High Damage PvE ✓ PvP ✓ DLC Compatible

⚔ Build Overview & Philosophy

The Samurai Bleed build is widely considered one of the strongest archetypes in all of Elden Ring — not just in the base game, but including Shadow of the Erdtree. It combines the hemorrhage status effect (which removes a flat percentage of an enemy's maximum HP on trigger) with fast katana attack strings and high Arcane investment to cycle procs at an almost obscene rate.

The build works by stacking blood loss buildup on enemies through rapid hits. Once the buildup bar fills, Hemorrhage triggers — dealing approximately 15% of the target's max HP as an instantaneous burst. Since this is percentage-based, it scales lethally against every boss in the game regardless of their HP pool. Stack two procs back to back and you've ripped 30% off a boss health bar in seconds.

Core Mechanic
Arcane is the primary stat for bleed builds — not Dexterity. Even though katanas often show only D-scaling in Arcane vs B-scaling in Dex, Arcane investment simultaneously boosts both weapon damage and your blood loss buildup value. Dexterity is a secondary investment. This is the single most important thing to understand.

Three Primary Build Paths

Classic Dual Katana

Best for: New players, early-mid game, learning the build. Dual wield Uchigatana + Uchigatana (or Uchi + Nagakiba) with Blood affinity and Seppuku. Powerstance L1 attacks hit twice simultaneously, doubling bleed buildup per swing.

This is the recommended starting path. You can run this all the way to the final boss with only modest investment.

Sanguine Samurai (Rivers of Blood)

Best for: Endgame, min-maxed damage, boss shredding. Rivers of Blood's Corpse Piler skill deals fire + physical damage, applies bleed, and hits 5 times in quick succession — even from mid-range. Two activations often equals two Hemorrhage procs on a boss.

Obtained late game (Mountaintops of Giants). This is the top-tier variant once unlocked.

Nagakiba Quality Build

Best for: Players who want excellent reach, PvP viability, and shield-breaking. Nagakiba is the longest katana in the game. With Seppuku and powerstanced with Uchigatana, it has exceptional range advantage. Splits investment between Strength and Dexterity (quality) rather than going heavy Arcane.

Pros & Cons

Strengths

Percentage-based Hemorrhage damage shreds bosses regardless of HP pool

Fast attack speed means constant buildup pressure

Multiple viable weapons at every stage of the game

Extremely fun and aggressive playstyle with big dramatic proc moments

Scales excellently into Shadow of the Erdtree

Weaknesses

Bleed buildup decays if you stop attacking — you must maintain pressure

Some late-game bosses are immune or highly resistant

Runs no shield — requires solid dodge timing

FP hungry when spamming Corpse Piler (Rivers of Blood path)

Rivers of Blood is late-game locked — early game needs patience

◈ Starting Class & Stat Distribution

Recommended Class: Samurai. No other starting class matches this for a bleed build. The Samurai starts with the Uchigatana (a bleed-capable weapon), strong Dexterity, and respectable Vigor. The starting Land of Reeds armor is functional, and you have a Longbow for pulling enemies. This is not a close contest.

Stat Softcaps
Vigor: Hard diminishing returns after 60 — aim for 40–60 depending on build level. Dexterity: Softcap at 80, but meaningful returns end around 60 for this build. Arcane: Good scaling through 50-60, tapers beyond. After ~45 Dex and ~50 Arc, further points into Faith (for Golden Vow at 25) often outperform.

Stat Priority Order (Dex/Arc Path)

  • 1
    Vigor to 30 immediately. You die constantly at low Vigor. Nothing else matters if you're one-shot. Hit this before anything else.
  • 2
    Dexterity to 30. Gets your weapon damage up fast and makes the Uchigatana feel strong. Add Endurance as needed to medium roll (under 70% equip load).
  • 3
    Arcane to 40, Dexterity to 40. Mid-game. This is when bleed really starts to feel oppressive. Apply Blood affinity to your weapon at this point if you haven't already.
  • 4
    Vigor to 40–50, Arcane to 50, Dexterity to 48. Endgame stats. At this point consider Faith to 15 for Flame, Grant Me Strength.
  • 5
    Faith to 25 (optional but recommended). After Arcane 50 and Dex ~45, the marginal damage from more Dex/Arc is low. 25 Faith unlocks Golden Vow (+15% damage). Often more efficient.

Affinity — Blood vs Occult
Apply Blood affinity to your katanas for maximum hemorrhage buildup. Blood affinity adds Arcane scaling and increases blood loss buildup on the weapon. Occult affinity also scales with Arcane and can rival Blood for pure AR on certain weapons — test both at your current stat spread. For the classic Samurai bleed build, Blood is almost always correct.

✦ Ashes of War

Priority Acquisitions

S
Seppuku
Self-buff · Blood Affinity Compatible

The single most important Ash of War in this build. Stabs the weapon into your own body, applying a blood buff that dramatically increases the weapon's bleed buildup for a limited duration and adds some extra damage. Always apply before tough fights. Reapply when the buff fades.

Note: it was nerfed in patch 1.06 — the buff duration and buildup bonus were reduced. It is still excellent and mandatory. Just don't expect the pre-nerf absurdity.

◉ Talismans

Core Four (Rivers of Blood / Dex-Arc Build)

Lord of Blood's Exultation
+20% attack power for 20 seconds whenever any blood loss occurs nearby. This procs constantly with this build — on enemies you damage, on spirits you summon, on yourself after Seppuku. Effectively a permanent 20% attack bonus in any serious fight.
📍 Leyndell Catacombs (underground Leyndell). Chest past the boss.
Rotten Winged Sword Insignia
Greatly raises attack power with successive attacks (stacking buff, 3 tiers). Since Corpse Piler and dual-katana strings hit 5+ times in a single combo, this builds up immediately and provides a massive damage multiplier on the later hits.
📍 Millicent's questline reward — complete her quest through the Scarlet Rot region to the end.
Shard of Alexander
+15% skill damage. Since Corpse Piler is your primary attack and Seppuku is a skill — this is a direct, reliable 15% boost to your most used abilities. Mandatory for the Rivers of Blood variant.
📍 Iron Fist Alexander questline reward. Complete his full questline through Jarburg and Crumbling Farum Azula.
Carian Filigreed Crest
Reduces FP cost of weapon skills. Corpse Piler is FP-hungry. Combined with Sacrificial Axe in off-hand (4 FP per kill), this lets you spam your primary attack without burning through Cerulean Tears. Swap for Green Turtle Talisman in longer boss fights.
📍 War Counselor Iji (northwest Liurnia). Available after triggering Blaidd's questline.

Equip Load Rule
Never exceed 69.9% of your max equip load. Medium roll (under 70%) gives you the large dodge roll with good iframes. Heavy roll (over 70%) gives you a fat roll with almost no iframes and you will die. Use Great-Jar's Arsenal Talisman if you need extra carry capacity.

☯ Combat Strategy

Core Principles

  • ▲
    Maintain pressure — bleed buildup decays. If you stop hitting an enemy for several seconds, their blood loss buildup gauge resets partway. You need to keep attacking. This means reading enemy animations for safe openings, not just trading.
  • ▲
    Preemptive Corpse Piler. The first swing of Corpse Piler is slow. Start the animation before the enemy enters range — the first hit will miss, but you'll be deep into the faster second combo by the time they arrive. Hitting two swings often procs Hemorrhage immediately.
  • ▲
    Use Lord of Blood's Exultation windows. After Hemorrhage procs, you have 20 seconds of +20% attack. Unleash your heaviest combos in this window. Then re-establish bleed for the next proc before the buff expires.
  • ▲
    Against large enemies: hug the chest. Large bosses have wide arm and tail sweeps but slow body turns. Get in close to their chest/stomach and attack upward — most of their swings will miss while you connect freely.
  • ▲
    No poise, no staggers from bleed attacks. You don't stagger enemies with bleed weapons. The Hemorrhage proc causes a brief interrupt, but during the combo itself you can be hit. Time your runs in and dodge out before committing to full strings if in doubt.

Powerstancing Mechanics (Dual Katana)

To powerstance, equip the same weapon type (katana + katana) in both hands. Your L1 button now executes a dual-wield attack — hitting with both weapons simultaneously. This doubles your bleed buildup per swing and makes Hemorrhage procs happen in 2-3 hits. The tradeoff is stamina cost per attack is higher.

Jump Attack Priority
Jump attacks deal excellent stance damage and accelerate bleed buildup. With Raptor's Black Feathers chest (+10%) and Claw Talisman (+15%), you're getting +25% jump attack damage. In early and mid game before you have Seppuku, opening fights with repeated jump attacks is extremely effective.

☠ Boss Immunity & Resistance Notes

Not every boss in Elden Ring is susceptible to bleed. Know this before going in.

  • Elden Beast — IMMUNE. Switch to pure physical/magic for this fight.
  • Malenia, Blade of Miquella — Weak to bleed despite being the bleed boss. Rivers of Blood Corpse Piler is still one of the best strategies.
  • Commander Niall — Immune to bleed.
  • Maliketh — High resistance, not immune. Still viable but slow procs.
  • Radagon — Susceptible. Bleed + fire damage from Rivers of Blood hits both damage types well.
  • Morgott — Susceptible. Good bleed target.
  • Rykard — Take Serpent-Hunter (provided in arena) but bleed works on him regardless.
  • Placidusax — High resistance. Still viable but consider weapon swap.
  • Margit / Godrick — Both highly susceptible. Great for testing the build.
  • Most field bosses, dungeon bosses — Highly susceptible. Bleed dominates open-world content.
Against Elden Beast
The Elden Beast (second phase of the final boss) is immune to both bleed and other status effects. During this fight, raw weapon AR matters. Consider keeping an Occult or Blood Rivers of Blood (physical/fire portion still works) but don't rely on Hemorrhage. The buffs (Flame, Grant Me Strength + Golden Vow) still apply.

Malenia Strategy (Rivers of Blood)

Rivers of Blood Corpse Piler became famous specifically because of Malenia. She is designed around bleed — her healing on hit is difficult to counteract — but the trick is: Corpse Piler's range lets you initiate from mid-range, bypassing her aggressive combo starters. Start the Corpse Piler animation just before she closes, catching the faster portion of the combo. Two activations deals two Hemorrhage procs and often kills her before she can heal more than once. Keep Black Knife Tiche alive as long as possible to split her attention.

✶ Shadow of the Erdtree

The bleed build remains one of the strongest archetypes in the DLC — arguably stronger than in the base game. The DLC introduces new weapons with innate bleed, higher-scaling Arcane gear, and enemies with substantial HP pools that make the percentage-based Hemorrhage mechanic even more potent.

Scadutree Fragments — Do This First
Scadutree Fragments are the DLC's scaling system. They increase your damage output and damage negation — up to 20 stacks. Without them, even a fully optimised bleed build will feel underpowered against DLC bosses. Collect as many as you can find before engaging any DLC content. They are scattered throughout the Realm of Shadow as glowing items and rewards.

DLC Boss Notes

Messmer the Impaler — Highly susceptible to bleed. His phase two adds delay animations designed to bait your combo timing. Wait for his combos to fully resolve before initiating Corpse Piler. Rivers of Blood mid-range initiations are particularly effective here. Two full procs typically ends phase one.

Romina, Saint of the Bud — Apply Seppuku before the fight and lead with Swarm of Flies for immediate buildup. She has wide sweep attacks but clear recovery windows. Her rot attacks can be avoided by staying close — the wide arc misses at melee range. Two Hemorrhage procs per phase is achievable with the classic Dex/Arc setup.

Scadutree Blessing Stacking
At maximum Scadutree stacks, your Hemorrhage proc value increases substantially because your base damage increases — and Hemorrhage is partly based on weapon AR in addition to the flat HP percentage. Stack Fragments aggressively: the DLC is balanced around ~10–15 stacks minimum for mid-DLC bosses.

◈ Quick Tips & Reference

Blood Affinity — Not Occult

Blood affinity adds both Arcane scaling AND increases blood loss buildup. Occult can match it in raw AR on some weapons but never beats it for bleed proc speed. Default to Blood unless testing proves otherwise.

Seppuku Timing

Apply Seppuku immediately before entering a boss fight, never mid-combat. The animation leaves you vulnerable. Budget ~30 seconds of uptime per application and reapply when the buff fades if the boss still lives.

Maintain Pressure — Buildup Decays

Blood loss buildup on enemies decays if you stop attacking for a few seconds. You must keep hitting. Read enemy animations for safe openings rather than waiting — this build punishes passivity.

Jump Attacks Stack Buildup

Jump attacks deal excellent stance damage and apply solid bleed. With Claw Talisman (+15%) and Raptor's Black Feathers (+10%), jump attacks become a 25% bonus damage option — strong for mid-game before Seppuku.

Corpse Piler Timing

The first hit of Corpse Piler is slow. Start the animation before the boss closes distance — you'll miss the first hit but land the faster follow-ups. Two activations typically procs Hemorrhage twice on a boss.

Hugging Large Bosses

Get in close to the chest of large enemies. Most of their arm and tail sweeps will miss entirely while you connect freely with katana attacks. This is especially effective on Dragon bosses.

White Mask + Lord of Blood

White Mask (+10% attack when blood loss occurs nearby) stacks with Lord of Blood's Exultation (+20%). After a Hemorrhage proc you have a combined ~32% attack bonus window — unleash your heaviest combos immediately.

Arcane over Dexterity at Endgame

After ~45 Dexterity, invest in Arcane first. It simultaneously raises weapon AR and blood loss buildup value. If you hit Arcane 50 and have Faith 15, consider Golden Vow (Faith 25) over further Dex — it often outperforms additional stat points.

Elden Beast — Drop Bleed

Elden Beast is fully immune to bleed. For this fight, rely on raw weapon AR and your buff stack (Flame Grant Me Strength + Golden Vow). Rivers of Blood's fire + physical portions still deal damage — just no Hemorrhage procs.
