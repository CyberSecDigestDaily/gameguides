Samurai Bleed Build

Rivers of Blood · Arcane Scaling · Corpse Piler · The fastest boss-killer in Elden Ring.

Arcane Dexterity Bleed PvE ✓ DLC Compatible

The Samurai Bleed Build is one of Elden Ring's most powerful PvE setups. It works by rapidly stacking blood loss on enemies using Rivers of Blood's Corpse Piler skill — a multi-hit ash of war that applies bleed buildup with each individual slash. When the bar fills, enemies take a percentage of their maximum HP as damage. Against bosses with large health pools, this is devastating.

This is not a passive or defensive playstyle. It rewards aggression and timing. Bait one boss attack, dodge into their recovery window, unload Corpse Piler, and disengage. The rhythm — dodge, punish, bleed proc, repeat — becomes second nature quickly and makes even the game's hardest bosses manageable.

This guide covers three distinct approaches: the Standard Rivers of Blood setup (peak bleed output, endgame ready), the Dual Wield variant (relentless pressure with dual katanas), and the Early Game Uchigatana setup to carry you until Rivers of Blood is available.

Strengths

  • Devastating bleed procs against most bosses
  • Corpse Piler hits multiple times — procs bleed fast
  • High DPS even outside bleed windows
  • Dual-wield option doubles bleed buildup speed
  • Excellent in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC
  • Samurai starting class makes early game easy

Weaknesses

  • Completely ineffective against bleed-immune enemies
  • Rivers of Blood only available mid-to-late game
  • Low poise — you must dodge, not tank hits
  • Relies heavily on one weapon skill
  • Can feel repetitive once you learn the loop

Build Variants

Three proven approaches to the bleed archetype. The core stat spread is identical across all three — only weapon pairing and ash of war changes. Choose based on your progression stage and preferred combat rhythm.

Standard — Rivers of Blood

The definitive version. Rivers of Blood with Corpse Piler in your right hand and an Uchigatana with Seppuku in your left. Apply Seppuku before each boss fight to boost bleed buildup, then open with Corpse Piler. One full sequence frequently procs bleed before the boss can recover. This is peak bleed output once you reach Mountaintops of the Giants.

Stats & Levelling

The Samurai is the optimal starting class. It begins with a well-distributed Dex/Arc spread, comes with an Uchigatana already in hand, and has enough starting Vigor to survive early areas without immediate investment. Arcane is your primary damage driver — it scales Rivers of Blood directly and increases bleed buildup per hit.

Key Breakpoints

Dexterity 18: Rivers of Blood minimum requirement — hit this before acquiring the weapon. Arcane 45: first significant bleed buildup scaling improvement. Arcane 60: endgame soft cap — push here before the DLC. Vigor 60: mandatory before any endgame boss.

Levelling Roadmap

🌱 Early Game — Levels 1–40

Priority order: Vigor 30 → Dexterity 18 → Arcane 30 → Endurance 20 → Faith 15

Push Vigor to 30 first — this build has no poise and you die fast. Hit Dexterity 18 as soon as possible so you can wield Rivers of Blood the moment you reach Mountaintops. Faith 15 unlocks Flame, Grant Me Strength. Run your Blood-infused Uchigatana through this entire phase.

⚔️ Mid Game — Levels 40–80

Priority order: Arcane 45 → Vigor 50 → Dexterity 30 → Endurance 25

Rivers of Blood is in your hands now. Arcane 45 is the first major bleed breakpoint — Corpse Piler will proc blood loss measurably faster. Push Vigor to 50 before Leyndell or Mountaintops bosses. Dexterity 30 adds meaningful physical damage.

🔥 End Game — Levels 80–150

Priority order: Vigor 60 → Arcane 60 → Dexterity 40 → Endurance 30

Vigor 60 is non-negotiable — you cannot take hits with this build. Arcane 60 is the soft cap for Rivers of Blood damage and bleed buildup rate. Dexterity 40 is the physical damage soft cap. After level 150 returns diminish sharply.

The Arcane Priority Rule

Arcane is unusual — it increases the bleed buildup value on your weapon per hit, not just your damage number. Every point in Arcane means fewer hits are required to proc blood loss. This compounds significantly at 45+ where Corpse Piler can proc bleed twice in a single skill sequence against large bosses.

Ashes of War

Must-Have · Rivers of Blood Only
Corpse Piler

The engine of this entire build. Fires a rapid flurry of blood slashes that each independently apply bleed buildup. Hold the button for the full multi-hit sequence. At medium range, all slashes connect — a single full activation can proc bleed twice against large or slow targets. Cannot be removed from Rivers of Blood.

FP Cost: 11 per cast · Best range: 2–4 metres from target · Hold for full combo
Pre-Fight Buff · Essential
Seppuku

Plunges the weapon into your own stomach, coating it in blood and dramatically increasing bleed buildup per hit for approximately 60 seconds. More importantly, the buff affects all sources of bleed — including Rivers of Blood. Activate this on your off-hand weapon before every fog gate. Equipment Load Target

Stay below 70% equip load at all times. Medium roll (30–70%) is mandatory for this build. A fat roll leaves you on the ground and gets you killed — this build has no shield to absorb hits in a pinch. Check your load after equipping weapons. If you're over 70%, reduce armour weight or use Erdtree's Favour +2 to raise your load limit.

Talismans

You have four talisman slots at endgame. Two are non-negotiable — Lord of Blood's Exultation and Millicent's Prosthesis. The remaining two flex between Shard of Alexander for skill damage and the Rotten Winged Sword Insignia for sustained attack builds.

Core Four — Standard Bleed Build

SLOT 1 — ESSENTIAL
Lord of Blood's Exultation

Raises attack power by 20% for 20 seconds whenever blood loss occurs nearby — including your own procs. Since you trigger bleed constantly, this buff is nearly permanent in any bleed-viable fight. The single most important talisman for this build.

📍 Leyndell Catacombs — dropped by Esgar, Priest of Blood [wiki ↗]

SLOT 2 — ESSENTIAL
Millicent's Prosthesis

Raises attack power by up to 8% on successive hits (stacks 5 times) and boosts Dexterity by 5 points. Both bonuses apply simultaneously. The multi-hit nature of Corpse Piler builds stacks almost instantly, and the Dex bonus is free scaling on Rivers of Blood.

📍 Millicent's questline reward — Mountaintops of the Giants [wiki ↗]

SLOT 3 — CORE
Shard of Alexander

Increases the damage of skills — specifically Ashes of War — by 15%. Corpse Piler is an Ash of War. This is a direct, unconditional 15% Corpse Piler damage increase. No conditions, no timing, no stacks. Just more damage on every cast of your main skill.

📍 End of Iron Fist Alexander's questline — Crumbling Farum Azula [wiki ↗]

SLOT 4 — FLEXIBLE
Rotten Winged Sword Insignia

Raises attack power with successive hits — up to +13% at maximum stacks. Stacks additively with Millicent's Prosthesis for a combined maximum of +21%. Rewards the sustained multi-hit pressure of Corpse Piler spam and dual L1 chains. Use instead of Shard of Alexander in the dual-wield variant.

📍 Millicent's questline — drops if you invade and kill her (Haligtree area) [wiki ↗]

Buffs, Consumables & Flask

Incantations

Best Offensive Buff
Flame, Grant Me Strength

Boosts physical and fire attack by 20% for 30 seconds. Rivers of Blood deals both physical and fire damage — both halves benefit. Requires Faith 15 which the Samurai starting class naturally reaches. Use before every boss fight. Short duration means you need to re-engage quickly after the animation.

General Purpose
Golden Vow

Increases damage by 15% and reduces damage taken by 10% for 80 seconds. The longer duration makes it more practical as a sustained buff during long boss phases. Requires Faith 25 — only worth investing if you plan to use multiple incantations. Otherwise Flame, Grant Me Strength is the better single-buff choice.

⚠️ Seppuku is a buff — use it before every fog gate

Seppuku on your off-hand weapon should be activated before every major fight, treating it the same way you'd drink a consumable buff. Activate it while waiting at the fog gate after casting your incantations. The self-inflict animation takes about 1.5 seconds. The bleed boost it applies to all your weapons is significant enough that skipping it noticeably reduces your damage output.

Combat Guide

The Core Loop

Every fight follows the same rhythm: bait one boss attack → dodge into the recovery window → activate Corpse Piler → follow up with 2–3 light attacks → disengage before the next attack. The key word is patience. New players panic-cast Corpse Piler before the boss is committed to an attack and get clipped mid-animation. Wait until you see the attack begin, then dodge through it. The recovery window that follows is your moment.

Dodge Timing — Into the Attack, Not Away

Dodge towards the boss and to their offhand side — this avoids most attacks and deposits you directly at their flank, in ideal range for Corpse Piler. Dodging away from attacks is reactive and puts you out of range for a follow-up. Dodging into attacks is aggressive and places you exactly where you want to be.

Pre-Fight Setup — Do This Every Time

Before entering any fog gate, complete this sequence: cast Flame, Grant Me Strength → activate Seppuku on your off-hand weapon → summon your spirit ash → drink Flask of Wondrous Physick. You enter the fight with boosted physical and fire attack, enhanced bleed buildup, an aggro-drawing summon, and the Thorny Cracked Tear buff active. Your first Corpse Piler sequence will hit harder than anything the boss is prepared for.

Combat Priority List

  • 1Cast Flame, Grant Me Strength and activate Seppuku at the fog gate before entering.
  • 2Summon your spirit ash immediately — give it one second to establish aggro before you move in.
  • 3Drink Flask of Wondrous Physick — Thorny Cracked Tear activates on your first hit.
  • 4Bait the boss's first attack combo. Dodge into the last hit of the string, landing at their flank.
  • 5Activate Corpse Piler — hold for the full combo. Stay at medium range so all slashes connect.
  • 6Follow up with 2–3 light attacks to maintain Insignia talisman stacks.
  • 7Disengage when stamina drops below 50%. Bleed proc gives you the Lord of Blood's Exultation window — use it to press the attack immediately after each proc.
Bleed-Immune Enemies — Switch Approach

Skeletons, certain undead, and a small number of bosses (including Mohg, Lord of Blood himself) are fully immune to blood loss. Against these enemies, Corpse Piler still deals physical and fire damage — it's not useless — but you lose the proc bonus. Switch to standard R1 combos, jump attacks, and rely on Unsheathe if you have it. The build still deals competitive physical damage without bleed.

Acquisition Route

Rivers of Blood is not available at the start of the game — it requires reaching the late-mid game area of Mountaintops of the Giants. Follow this route to unlock the full build efficiently. The Uchigatana with Seppuku bridges the early and mid-game with minimal compromise to effectiveness.

Route Overview

Limgrave (start, Uchigatana) → Liurnia (level, begin Millicent questline) → Altus Plateau (push Arcane, collect Rotten Winged Sword Insignia) → Mountaintops of the Giants (Rivers of Blood, Seppuku AoW) → Leyndell Catacombs (Lord of Blood's Exultation) → Crumbling Farum Azula (Rivers of Blood +10, Shard of Alexander)

Step-by-Step

  • 1Limgrave: Start as Samurai with your Uchigatana. Push Vigor to 30 immediately. Infuse Uchigatana with Blood affinity once you reach a Site of Grace with the Whetstone Knife. Begin levelling Arcane and Dexterity as your secondary priority.
  • 2Liurnia of the Lakes: Begin Millicent's questline by speaking to Gowry southeast of Aeonia Swamp. Complete Stormveil normally or use the Liurnia shortcut. Push Dexterity to 18 here — you need this to wield Rivers of Blood the moment you acquire it.
  • 3Altus Plateau: Reach via Grand Lift of Dectus or Ruin-Strewn Precipice. Continue Millicent's questline for Rotten Winged Sword Insignia. Push Arcane to 40+. Complete Leyndell to open access to Mountaintops.
  • 4Mountaintops of the Giants: Reach via Grand Lift of Rold after defeating Morgott. Rivers of Blood drops from Bloody Finger Okina — he invades near Church of Repose. Use Furlcalling Finger Remedy to see his summon sign. Kill him to permanently obtain Rivers of Blood. Seppuku AoW is found nearby on a frozen lake.
  • 5Leyndell Catacombs: Complete this dungeon to defeat Esgar, Priest of Blood and collect Lord of Blood's Exultation. Short dungeon — well worth the detour. Can be done before or after the Mountaintops.
  • 6Crumbling Farum Azula: Late-game area after Maliketh. Complete Iron Fist Alexander's questline for Shard of Alexander. Upgrade Rivers of Blood to +10 with the Somber Ancient Dragon Stone found here. The build is now complete.

Boss-Specific Tips

Margit the Fell Omen — First Major Test

Bleed procs quickly on Margit. His combo strings are long but well-telegraphed — dodge the final hit of each string and land Corpse Piler into his recovery. He will stagger after two or three consecutive procs. The only unusual attack is his Erdtree seal slam — if he raises it above his head in a slow wind-up, dodge backward, not into him. Everything else, dodge and punish.

Malenia, Blade of Miquella — This Build's Showcase Fight

Malenia is the bleed build's best fight in the game. She heals on every attack that lands — but Corpse Piler deals enough damage per activation to far outpace her healing. Dodge the Waterfowl Dance (her most dangerous attack — dodge away from the first flurry, then roll into the second). After she lands, immediately activate Corpse Piler. She will proc bleed within the first two Corpse Piler activations. The fight becomes a DPS race you are extremely well-equipped to win.

Malenia's Lifesteal — Outpace It

Malenia heals approximately 300–500 HP per hit she lands. With Lord of Blood's Exultation active and Arcane at 60, a single bleed proc deals 15% of her max HP — roughly 2,000–3,000 damage in one moment. Two procs per phase is enough to win the DPS race comfortably. Don't panic about the healing. Focus on landing clean Corpse Piler windows.

Mohg, Lord of Blood — Ironic Immunity

Mohg is immune to blood loss — ironic for a blood god. Corpse Piler still deals physical and fire damage so it's not useless, but you lose all proc bonuses. Focus on physical damage. Dodge his Nihil ritual (three hits with the trident held overhead) and use Morse Code Cracked Tear to negate its HP drain. After phase two begins, Black Knife Tiche is ideal — she avoids his large AoE attacks far better than melee summons.

Maliketh, the Black Blade — Speed and Patience

Maliketh is very fast and bleed works on him. The challenge is landing Corpse Piler without getting clipped — his attacks have deceptive range. Summon Mimic Tear to split aggro. Wait for him to land from a leap or finish a combo string, then activate Corpse Piler once from medium range. Never greedily follow up — one skill sequence, disengage, wait for the next window.

Shadow of the Erdtree

The bleed build is exceptional in the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC — arguably better here than in the base game. DLC enemies have higher HP pools, which amplifies percentage-based bleed proc damage. Rivers of Blood +10 with Arcane 60 and full talisman setup clears DLC content efficiently once you have Scadutree Fragments stacked.

Scadutree Fragments — Collect These First

Scadutree Fragments are the DLC's power scaling system. They directly increase your damage output and damage negation up to 20 stacks. Without them, even this fully optimised bleed build will struggle against DLC bosses — they hit harder and have significantly more HP than base game equivalents. Prioritise collecting Fragments in each new area before engaging bosses.

New DLC Tools for Bleed Builds

The DLC introduces new weapons and ashes of war with innate bleed or bleed-adjacent mechanics. Several new katanas have higher Arcane scaling than the base game options, and there are new curved swords with bleed buildup that pair well with the existing Rivers of Blood setup. Explore new areas before committing — some of the best DLC weapons for bleed are hidden in optional dungeons.

DLC Boss Notes

Messmer the Impaler is the DLC's hardest fight for bleed builds. He is not immune to bleed, but his aggressive fire-focused attacks chip through your dodges constantly. Summon Black Knife Tiche — her mobility lets her survive his AoE attacks where Mimic Tear often dies. Equip the Fire Drake Talisman. Bait his standard slash combos for Corpse Piler windows and avoid activating it during his fire burst sequences. His second phase adds delayed detonations — wait for them before committing.

Quick Tips & Reference

Seppuku Before Every Boss

Treat Seppuku like a consumable buff. Activate it on your off-hand at the fog gate every single time. It meaningfully increases bleed buildup and costs nothing except 11 FP and 1.5 seconds.

Dodge Into Attacks

Dodge towards the boss and to their offhand side, not away. This avoids most attacks and puts you in Corpse Piler range immediately after the dodge resolves. Dodging away puts you out of position.

Hold for Full Corpse Piler

Hold the skill button for the complete slash sequence — don't tap it. The full combo lands 6–8 hits. Each hit builds bleed. Tapping gives you 2–3 hits. One full sequence often procs bleed; a tap rarely does.

Press After Every Bleed Proc

When a proc triggers, your Lord of Blood's Exultation activates for 20 seconds. Use this window to press hard — land another Corpse Piler immediately and start building toward the next proc. Chain procs are devastating.

Medium Roll at All Times

Stay below 70% equip load. You have no shield. If you fat-roll you will die. Check your load after equipping dual katanas. Use Erdtree's Favor +2 or remove heavy armour pieces to stay in medium range.

Upgrade Rivers of Blood First

Every Somber Smithing Stone you find goes into Rivers of Blood. It outpaces your off-hand weapon in every fight. A +6 Rivers of Blood with Arcane scaling is dramatically stronger than a +0 one. Don't split upgrades early.

Bleed Immune? Still Use Corpse Piler

Against bleed-immune enemies, Corpse Piler still deals physical and fire damage. It won't proc blood loss but it's still decent damage. Switch focus to sustained light attacks and jump attacks rather than relying solely on the skill.

Scadutree Fragments — DLC

Collect every Scadutree Fragment before engaging DLC bosses. Bleed proc damage scales with enemy max HP — which DLC bosses have a lot of. But you still need the Fragment bonuses to avoid being one-shot by their hits.

Arcane is Your Primary Stat

Arcane directly increases bleed buildup per hit and scales Rivers of Blood damage. Push it to 60 before spending levels anywhere else at endgame. Dex is important but Arcane is what makes this build dominant.
