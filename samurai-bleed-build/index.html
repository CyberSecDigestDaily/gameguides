Overview

The Samurai Bleed Build is one of Elden Ring's most dominant PvE setups. It combines the bleed proc mechanic with Arcane scaling and the Corpse Piler skill from Rivers of Blood to melt bosses and enemies that aren't immune to blood loss. Once you understand the rhythm of baiting attacks and unleashing your ash of war, this build makes even the hardest fights feel manageable.

Strengths

Weaknesses

  • Can feel repetitive in gameplay loop

💡 How Bleed Works

Bleed procs when the bleed buildup bar on an enemy fills to 100%. Each proc deals a flat percentage of the enemy's max HP — typically 10–15%. Against high-HP bosses this is enormous. Arcane scaling increases the bleed buildup rate per hit, meaning fewer hits are needed per proc.

Build Variants

This build has three main configurations. Each suits different playstyles and progression stages. The core stat spread remains the same — only the weapon pairing and Ash of War changes.

Standard — Rivers of Blood

The definitive version. Rivers of Blood with Corpse Piler in your right hand and either the Uchigatana or Nagakiba in your left for dual-wield L1 attacks. This is peak bleed output once you reach Mountaintops of the Giants.

Stats & Levelling

Arcane is the primary investment for this build — it directly scales Rivers of Blood's damage and increases bleed buildup per hit. Dexterity is your secondary stat, as Rivers of Blood has a Dex requirement and Dex increases cast speed. Endurance is important early to allow medium-load dodging; once you hit softcaps elsewhere, pump Vigor to 60 for endgame survivability.

⚠ Levelling Order

Early game: prioritise Vigour to 40, Endurance to 25, then push Arcane to 45 and Dexterity to 18 (Rivers of Blood requirement). Once you have the weapon, push Arcane to 55+ and Dex to 40. Vigour last push to 60 before endgame bosses.

Weapons

Rivers of Blood is the centrepiece. It cannot be infused or have its Ash of War changed — Corpse Piler is locked in. Upgrade it to +10 as soon as possible with Somber Smithing Stones. Your off-hand weapon should complement the bleed gameplan.

Primary Weapons

Rivers of Blood
Main Hand Somber +10

Arcane scaling · Corpse Piler · Cannot be infused

The core of this build. Corpse Piler fires multiple slashes in a wide arc, each dealing physical and fire damage while stacking bleed at a massive rate. Use it to proc bleed quickly then continue attacking normally for bleed resets.

Uchigatana
Off Hand Standard +25

Blood affinity · Seppuku AoW · Dex/Arc scaling

The classic Samurai starting weapon. Infused with Blood affinity and Seppuku, it adds bleed to your left hand and — crucially — the Seppuku self-buff also boosts your Rivers of Blood bleed buildup while active.

Nagakiba
Off Hand Alt Standard +25

Longest katana reach · Blood affinity

Longer reach than the Uchigatana, making it better for dual-wield L1 attacks where range matters. Equip with Blood Slash or Seppuku. A solid alternative if you prefer extended range over familiar starting weapon.

Ashes of War

Two skills drive this build. Corpse Piler is your primary damage tool — it's what makes Rivers of Blood tier S in PvE. Seppuku is your offensive buff, applied to the off-hand weapon before big fights to dramatically increase bleed buildup for a window of time.

Corpse Piler
Rivers of Blood Only

FP Cost: 11 per cast · Cannot be removed

Unleashes a rapid flurry of blood slashes in front of you. Each individual hit applies bleed buildup independently, meaning a single Corpse Piler sequence can proc bleed multiple times against large or slow enemies. Use it from medium distance so all slashes connect.

Seppuku
Off-Hand AoW

FP Cost: 11 · Duration: ~60 seconds

Plunges the weapon into your stomach, applying a persistent bleed buff that increases your bleed buildup per hit significantly. Use this before entering any boss fight — the animation is quick and the buff duration is generous. Reapply when it expires.

✅ Optimal Pre-Boss Routine

Before entering any fog gate: activate Flame, Grant Me Strength (incantation), then activate Seppuku on your off-hand weapon. You'll enter the boss fight with higher attack power and a bleed buff active. Your first Corpse Piler sequence will proc bleed almost immediately on most bosses.

Armour

Armour for this build is primarily chosen to maintain medium load for the fast roll while hitting a useful level of physical and fire defence. The White Reed Set is the thematic choice as the Samurai armour set from the base game, but there are stronger options for pure performance.

Recommended Sets

White Reed Set
Thematic Lightweight

Weight: 17.2 · Good Dex/balanced defence

The classic Samurai armour set found in Spiritcaller's Cave. Lightweight and looks great. Not the best raw defence, but carries the aesthetic perfectly and keeps you well under medium load even with heavier weapons.

Raptor's Black Feathers
Performance Chest

Chest only — boosts jump attack damage

This chest piece provides a hidden damage bonus to jump attacks. Pair it with any combination of lighter head/leg/hand pieces to stay under medium load. Jump attacks apply bleed buildup twice per hit on some frames — the damage boost compounds significantly.

Bloodsoaked Set
Bleed-Themed Medium Weight

Weight: 22.3 · Solid all-round defence

Found in the Volcano Manor area. Heavier than the White Reed Set but provides notably better damage negation. Use this if you find yourself dying frequently and want more survivability without sacrificing the bleed aesthetic of the build.

Talismans

All four talisman slots contribute directly to bleed damage output or survivability. Two of the four are essentially mandatory — Lord of Blood's Exultation and Millicent's Prosthesis. The remaining two slots offer flexibility depending on the fight.

1

Lord of Blood's Exultation

Raises attack power by 20% for 20 seconds whenever blood loss occurs nearby. Since you proc bleed constantly, this buff is nearly permanent in any bleed-viable fight. Non-negotiable for this build.

Location: Leyndell Catacombs — dropped by Esgar, Priest of Blood

2

Millicent's Prosthesis

Raises attack power by up to 8% per successive hit (stacks 5 times), and boosts Dexterity by 5 points. Both benefits apply — the stacking attack boost rewards the relentless multi-hit nature of Corpse Piler and rapid L1 attacks.

Location: Millicent's questline — reward for completing her questline in Mountaintops of the Giants

3

Rotten Winged Sword Insignia

Raises attack power with successive hits, up to +13% at maximum stacks. Synergises with Millicent's Prosthesis — the two effects stack together for a combined attack bonus that rewards sustained combat pressure rather than burst-and-retreat.

Location: Millicent's questline — drops from Millicent if you chose to invade and kill her

4

Shard of Alexander

Increases the damage of skills — specifically Ashes of War — by 15%. Corpse Piler is an Ash of War. This is a direct, unconditional Corpse Piler damage boost. If you've completed Iron Fist Alexander's questline, slot this immediately.

Location: End of Iron Fist Alexander's questline — Crumbling Farum Azula

Buffs & Consumables

This build has access to powerful buff stacking. The combination of Seppuku, Flame Grant Me Strength, and a Flask of Wondrous Physick can create a short window of extreme damage output that will one-phase many bosses if executed at the right moment.

Core Buffs

Seppuku
Ash of War Buff

Duration: ~60 seconds · Reapplicable

Apply to off-hand weapon before every major fight. Increases bleed buildup on all hits for the duration. Always active in any serious encounter.

Flame, Grant Me Strength
Incantation

Duration: 30 seconds · Faith 15 required

Boosts physical and fire attack by 20%. Rivers of Blood deals both physical and fire damage — both portions benefit. Requires 15 Faith, which this build typically hits naturally through the Samurai starting class.

Golden Vow
Incantation

Duration: 80 seconds · Faith 25 required

Boosts attack and defence by 7.5% and 10% respectively. Costs more Faith investment (25) but provides a longer duration buff. Useful if you've invested Faith for Dragon Communion incantations. Optional for standard builds.

Flask of Wondrous Physick
One-Use Per Rest

Customisable — see tear recommendations below

A powerful one-use consumable that combines two Crystal Tears for an effect. Use Thorny Cracked Tear (boosts successive hit damage) + Bloodsucking Cracked Tear (recovers HP on blood loss) for a devastating damage-sustain combo.

Spirit Summons

Spirit Ashes provide a crucial second presence in boss fights — they draw aggro, disrupt boss combos, and give you windows to unleash Corpse Piler safely. These are the top choices for a bleed build that wants maximum uptime on its offensive rotation.

Black Knife Tiche
S-Tier Somber +10

FP Cost: 132 · Requires Roderika upgrades

The single best summon for this build. Tiche is extremely mobile and hard for bosses to hit, meaning she survives long fights intact. Her Black Knife attacks deal percentage-based damage which scales into the endgame. She distracts bosses reliably without dying.

Mimic Tear
S-Tier (Burst) Somber +10

HP Cost: 660 · Copies your loadout

Mimic Tear copies your exact equipment at the moment of summon. With this bleed build, you get a second Rivers of Blood user. Two Corpse Piler casters double the bleed proc rate, often leading to near-instant blood loss on bosses. Only downside is it costs HP instead of FP, and it dies faster than Tiche.

Bloodhound Knight Floh
A-Tier Standard +10

FP Cost: 95 · Bleed-capable summon

An aggressive melee summon that applies bleed independently of your attacks. When Floh is in the fight, bleed procs happen faster because you have two sources of buildup. Very useful against bosses with high HP where proc speed is critical. More accessible than Tiche early in the game.

Latenna the Albinauric
A-Tier (Stationary) Standard +10

FP Cost: 74 · Ranged attacks only

A ranged summon that deals significant damage from a fixed position. Latenna will not reposition, so she's best used in arena-style boss fights where the boss stays in one area. Her arrow volleys apply solid pressure and deal good damage. Extremely effective against slower or larger boss types.

💡 When to Summon

Summon at the start of the fight, not after you're in trouble. Most summons draw aggro immediately — use that window to apply Seppuku and drink your Wondrous Physick before engaging. Your opening Corpse Piler sequence will land while the boss is focused on your summon.

Combat Guide

The core combat loop is simple once internalised, but takes practice to execute under pressure. The key is patience — don't panic-spam Corpse Piler as the first thing you do. Bait one or two attacks, dodge into the recovery window, then unleash your skill.

Standard Combat Loop

  1. 1Enter fight — summon spirit ash immediately to establish aggro split
  2. 2Apply Seppuku to off-hand weapon (takes ~1 second, do it while boss is occupied)
  3. 3Drink Flask of Wondrous Physick for the Thorny + Bloodsucking buff window
  4. 4Bait one boss attack — dodge into it (towards the boss, not away)
  5. 5During boss recovery frames: activate Corpse Piler (hold for full combo)
  6. 6Follow up with 2–3 light attacks to maintain hit-streak for Insignia talisman stacks
  7. 7Disengage and repeat — don't get greedy

When Bleed Procs

When a bleed proc happens, your Lord of Blood's Exultation talisman activates and your Bloodsucking Cracked Tear triggers. You get a 20% attack boost and recover some HP simultaneously. Use this window to press the attack — land another Corpse Piler immediately to start building toward the next proc. Chain procs are devastating.

Positioning Tips

  • Position yourself at medium range before firing Corpse Piler — all slashes need to connect to apply full bleed buildup
  • Dodge towards attacks and to the boss's offhand side — this avoids most attacks and puts you in ideal melee range
  • Use jump attacks to close distance quickly — especially effective with Raptor's Black Feathers equipped
  • If stamina runs low, disengage — never Corpse Piler with no stamina to dodge out of punishment

⚠ Bleed-Immune Enemies

A small number of enemies are fully immune to blood loss — primarily skeletons, some undead, and a handful of late-game bosses. Against these enemies, switch to normal katana attacks and rely on physical damage only. Keep the White Reed Set and a second weapon set ready. The build still deals competitive physical damage even without bleed procs.

Acquisition Route

Rivers of Blood is not available at the start of the game. Follow this route to unlock the full build as efficiently as possible. The Uchigatana is your bridge weapon until you reach Mountaintops of the Giants at approximately the halfway point of the game.

1. Limgrave

Start as Samurai — you begin with an Uchigatana already in hand. Infuse it with Blood affinity at a Site of Grace once you have the Ash of War: Seppuku (found in Mountaintops later, or buy from Twin Maiden Husks after finding the Bell Bearing). Push through Stormveil Castle and reach Liurnia of the Lakes.

2. Liurnia of the Lakes

Level Arcane to 20+ and Dexterity to 18 here. Buy the Academy Glintstone Key workaround or complete the normal path through Stormveil. Find Millicent's questline beginning — speak to Gowry in his shack southeast of the Aeonia Swamp. Her questline ultimately rewards both major talismans this build needs.

3. Altus Plateau

Reach Altus Plateau via the Grand Lift of Dectus (requires two halves of the Dectus Medallion) or via the Ruin-Strewn Precipice dungeon. Push Arcane to 40+ here. Collect the Rotten Winged Sword Insignia from Millicent's questline continuation in the Altus Plateau area.

4. Mountaintops of the Giants

Access via Grand Lift of Rold (requires Rold Medallion from Morgott boss fight). Rivers of Blood drops from Bloody Finger Okina, who invades your world near the Church of Repose on the Mountaintops. He only invades at human effigies — use a Furlcalling Finger Remedy to see him. Kill him to obtain Rivers of Blood permanently.

5. Leyndell Catacombs

Before or after Mountaintops — complete Leyndell Catacombs to defeat Esgar, Priest of Blood and collect Lord of Blood's Exultation. This dungeon is accessible once you reach the Leyndell, Royal Capital area. It's a short dungeon — well worth the detour before tackling any major boss.

6. Crumbling Farum Azula

Late-game area accessible after completing Maliketh, the Black Blade. Complete Iron Fist Alexander's questline here to receive the Shard of Alexander talisman — the final piece of the optimised build. Upgrade Rivers of Blood to +10 with the Somber Ancient Dragon Stone found in this area.

Bleed Immunity List

The following enemy types are fully immune to blood loss. Switch playstyle against these — focus on physical damage and normal combos rather than Corpse Piler spam:

  • Skeletons and undead (most variants)
  • Mohg, Lord of Blood
  • Some Pureblood Knight enemies
  • Certain late-game constructs and stone golems
  • Some Crucible Knight variants

Shadow of the Erdtree DLC

The Shadow of the Erdtree expansion adds powerful new weapons and challenges for the bleed build. The DLC content is locked behind defeating Mohg, Lord of Blood in the base game first — which is ironic given Mohg is immune to bleed. Once accessed, the DLC rewards this build heavily.

DLC Highlights for This Build

Euporia
DLC Weapon Str/Dex/Arc

Bleed buildup · Unique Ash of War

A curved greatsword with built-in bleed. Slower than katanas but each hit applies substantial bleed buildup. An interesting side-grade for players who want more range and poise-breaking power while keeping the bleed identity.

Smithscript Cirque
DLC Weapon Throwing Blade

Bleed · Ranged application

A throwing blade-type weapon that applies bleed at range. Useful for building up bleed on flying enemies or those that are difficult to approach. Pairs with the standard Rivers of Blood setup — swap in when melee range isn't practical.

Scadutree Fragments
DLC Power System

Up to +20% damage/defence in DLC areas

The DLC uses Scadutree Fragments as a power scaling system rather than character levels. Find and collect all fragments to maximise your damage and survivability in Shadow of the Erdtree. Without them, even this powerful build will struggle against DLC bosses.

Quick Tips

A condensed reference for players who already know the basics and need fast reminders during a session.

Pre-Boss Checklist

  • Seppuku active on off-hand weapon
  • Flame, Grant Me Strength cast
  • Wondrous Physick ready to drink
  • Spirit Ash selected and FP sufficient
  • Full health and enough flasks

Corpse Piler Tips

  • Stay at medium range before activating
  • Hold the button for the full combo
  • Use after a dodged boss attack
  • Don't use when stamina is low
  • One full combo can proc bleed twice on large bosses

Bleed Immune? Do This

  • Switch to standard R1/R2 combos
  • Jump attacks still deal good physical damage
  • Corpse Piler still deals fire damage — still usable
  • Focus on aggressive physical pressure
  • Consider swapping Exultation talisman temporarily

Stat Priority Reminder

  • Vigour to 50 before endgame zones
  • Arcane is your primary damage driver
  • Dex to 40 minimum (Rivers of Blood req.)
  • Don't neglect Endurance — stamina matters
  • Mind only needs 20 for FP sustain

✅ Build Summary

Rivers of Blood +10 with Corpse Piler in main hand. Uchigatana (Blood) with Seppuku in off hand. Arcane 60, Dex 40, Vigour 60. Talismans: Lord of Blood's Exultation, Millicent's Prosthesis, Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, Shard of Alexander. Buff with Seppuku + Flame Grant Me Strength before every boss. Summon Black Knife Tiche or Mimic Tear. Dodge into attacks, punish with Corpse Piler.
