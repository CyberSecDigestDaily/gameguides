Build Introduction

The Blessed Hammer Paladin is one of the strongest S-Tier leveling builds in Diablo 4 Season 13. You throw spiralling hammers that orbit your character and deal massive Holy damage while maintaining near-permanent Arbiter Form through a rotation of Disciple cooldown skills. The result is a build that feels godlike from the very first levels and only gets stronger as you invest skill points.

This build uses the Disciple Oath, which transforms you into an angelic Arbiter form for 4.5 seconds each time you activate a Disciple cooldown skill — granting 50% increased damage while active. With multiple Disciple skills on your bar, you can chain these activations to maintain Arbiter Form 100% of the time in combat.

💡
Season 13 Note: The Skill Tree has been redesigned in the Lord of Hatred expansion. Passive nodes are removed — skills are now unlocked by character level rather than the number of points invested in prior tiers. Upgrade modifiers are purchased directly on each skill.

Paladin Class Mechanic – Oaths

Paladins choose one of four Oaths at the start of their journey. Each Oath fundamentally changes your gameplay and resource access. For the Blessed Hammer build, take the Disciple Oath.

⚜ Disciple Oath

Whenever you cast a Disciple skill with a cooldown, you enter Arbiter Form for 4.5 seconds. In Arbiter Form, your Wing Strikes gain Disciple Skill benefits and your Disciple Skills deal 50% increased damage. Since your skill bar is loaded with Disciple cooldown skills, you should maintain this form nearly 100% of combat time.

✦Arbiter Form duration: 4.5 seconds per Disciple cooldown cast
✦Disciple Skill damage bonus in Arbiter Form: +50%
✦Wing Strikes gain Disciple Skill benefits

Place one Ritual Rune and one Invocation Rune into a two-socket item (helm, chest, pants, or 2H weapon) to create a Runeword. Ritual Runes generate Offering; Invocation Runes trigger when the threshold is met.

Combine Moni + Gar in your helm or pants for consistent Critical Strike Chance uptime. Cir + Thul in a second piece gives you frequent Freeze and Vulnerable application to boost damage.

Talisman & Charms

The Talisman system is a new feature from the Lord of Hatred expansion. A Legendary Seal holds up to 5 Charms (6 with the +1 Charm Slot affix). The 5-piece Light's Epiphany charm set is mandatory for this build.

ℹ️
Target a Legendary Seal with +1 Charm Slots to access all 6 charm slots. During early leveling, use whatever Seal you find. Look for bonus affixes that enhance Light's Epiphany: damage reduction, Faith generation, movement speed, or resistances.

Light's Epiphany – 5-Piece Charm Set

2-Piece Bonus
Disciple Skills reduce the Cooldown of Arbiter of Justice by 0.1 seconds when they deal damage, or 0.5 seconds if they have a Cooldown.
3-Piece Bonus
While in Arbiter Form, gain 30% increased Maximum Life and 10%× increased Movement Speed.
5-Piece Bonus
When you cast Arbiter of Justice, unleash all Ultimates — triggering Heaven's Fury and Fortress — and replace Arbiter of Justice with Zenith for 10 seconds. Gain 500%× increased damage with Disciple Skills for the duration. All Ultimates are also Disciple Skills.

6th Charm Slot – Unique Charm

Fill the 6th slot with a Unique Charm carrying Griswold's Opus power. This grants you the sword's powerful bonus without equipping the weapon itself, freeing your weapon slot for a better Aspect and affixes.

Rotation & Playstyle

The playstyle is dynamic and fluid. Your primary goal is to maintain Arbiter Form 100% of combat time by chaining Disciple cooldown casts, while Blessed Hammer spirals out and destroys everything nearby.

🌟
1. Falling Star — Enter the Fight
Open every encounter by diving in with Falling Star. This deals heavy AoE Holy damage and immediately triggers Arbiter Form. Use additional charges to dash between enemy groups and keep the form refreshed.
🔨
2. Blessed Hammer — Core DPS Spam
While in Arbiter Form, spam Blessed Hammer constantly. The hammers spiral outward and follow you, making them excellent against dense packs. Cast this in every available global cooldown window.
⚖️
3. Condemn — Extend Arbiter + Control
Use Condemn immediately after Falling Star to re-trigger Arbiter Form before it expires. Condemn also pulls enemies together and stuns them, then applies Vulnerable — all stacking your damage further.
⚡
4. Rally — Faith & Speed
Use Rally freely for the Faith generation and movement speed boost. It also helps you reposition rapidly between packs. With Words of Sacrifice, Rally will cost Life instead of nothing — you have abundant healing to compensate.
🛡️
5. Defiance Aura — Passive + Emergency
Defiance Aura passively provides Armor, All Resistance, and generates Resolve stacks. Activate it proactively when expecting a burst of incoming damage — it makes you Unstoppable for 2 seconds and absorbs the spike.
⚔️
6. Advance — Gap Closer & Arbiter Refresh
Advance is your fallback basic skill. Use it early to generate Faith and later as a quick Disciple cooldown to re-enter Arbiter Form when other cooldowns are on cooldown. With Flash on the Blade (point 29+), it becomes much more impactful.

Resolve — Damage Reduction System

Resolve is the Paladin's primary defence system. Each stack provides a flat 20% damage reduction regardless of hit size. Getting struck by any enemy removes one stack.

ℹ️
Default maximum Resolve stacks: 8. This cap increases through Legendary Aspects (Glynn's Anvil: +2), Unique Items, and Tempering. With our Helm and Chest tempering (+2–3 each), you can push maximum Resolve to 14+ stacks, giving enormous sustained damage reduction.
